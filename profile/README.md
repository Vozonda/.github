<h3 align="center">Turn sources into your podcast</h3>
<p align="center"><em>Vozonda reads them, voices make waves, you listen.</em></p>

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://vozonda.com">vozonda.com</a> ·
  nostr <code>_@vozonda.com</code> ·
  open source · self-hosted
</p>

---

Vozonda turns articles, PDFs, images and notes into a two-voice podcast episode that lands in
your own podcast feed or on Nostr.

- **Yours to run.** Self-hosted with Docker, on your own hardware and your own model endpoints.
- **No accounts, no telemetry.** Your sources and episodes stay with you.
- **Grounded.** Every line of the script points back to the source it came from.
- **Open feeds.** A standard RSS feed with chapters and transcripts, plus Nostr publishing.
- **Lightning built in.** Value-for-value splits for listeners who want to support a show.

The name joins *voz* (voice) and *onda* (wave).

**Status:** the code is being prepared for its public release. Watch this organization or
[vozonda.com](https://vozonda.com) for the launch.
